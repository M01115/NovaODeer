Puff Sound Kit

Every moment in the app gets a sound from Puff's world: bubbles instead of beeps, a shell marimba instead of a xylophone, sonar pings for the timer, rubbery skin for inflating. Like Duolingo, the good moments rise in pitch and the "not yet" moments are soft, short, and never a buzzer. Every sound is made from code and sits in D major pentatonic, so any two can overlap without clashing.

Rising = goodSoft blub = not yetBig sounds match the animation timingsNo sound scolds
Files: wav/<id>.wav (44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono) plus sounds.json. In Expo, load with expo-audio and set the rate where a row says to (creak, word blip). To change a sound, edit its recipe in puff-sounds.js and run node render.js.
